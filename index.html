    Questions

    Is NetShield really free?

    Yes. There is no paid tier and no account to create.

    Will it slow down my browsing?

    No. Blocking unwanted requests usually makes pages load faster.

    Can I allow a site that gets blocked?

    Yes. Add it to your allow list and NetShield will let it through.
